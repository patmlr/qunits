qunits.prefix

The qunits.prefix module contains the SI prefixes, the exponent maps of the units and the Context of the units.

Exponent maps

An exponent map (ExpMap) is a frozenset of (symbol, exponent) pairs, e.g. frozenset({("m", 1), ("s", -1)}) for m/s. It identifies a unit and is how the unit is printed. Units are multiplied and divided by merging their exponent maps with merge_exp_maps.

Contexts

The Context of a unit distinguishes quantities of the same dimension that must not be mixed, e.g. angles from other dimensionless quantities. CONTEXT_DICT maps each context to the symbol of its unit, CONTEXT_DICT_UNIT the other way around.

Prefixes

PREFIX_DICT maps the prefix symbols to their exponents, PREFIX_DICT_EXP the other way around. The prefixes are written in ASCII, micro is u. Only the prefixes for powers of 1000 are defined.
