qunits. Quantity  (  value ,  unit=None  )[source]

A value with a Unit. The value is a float or a numpy array of floats. Quantities are usually created by multiplying a number or an array with a unit, e.g. 3 * u.m, and support:

  • + and -, also in place, with quantities of the same dimension and context. The result is in the unit of the left operand. Numbers and arrays can only be added to and subtracted from dimensionless quantities without context, e.g. 0.5 * u.m / u.km + 1.
  • * and /, also in place, with numbers, arrays, units and quantities.
  • np.add, np.subtract, np.multiply and np.divide, and np.sin, np.cos and np.tan of angles, i.e. dimensionless quantities with the context Context.ANGLE such as u.rad.

Other numpy functions and the methods reduce, accumulate, outer and at of the numpy functions, e.g. np.sum, raise a NotImplementedError.

Printed quantities show scalar values rounded to 15 significant digits, e.g. 5.225 km instead of 5.2250000000000005 km, which hides the rounding errors of the binary floats. The values themselves are not rounded. Arrays are printed with the precision of numpy.

Parameters:
valuearray_like | Quantity

The value, a number, an array-like or a quantity. Numbers are converted to float and array-likes to numpy arrays of float64.

unitUnit | None

The unit. A quantity value is converted to it. Defaults to the unit of a quantity value, and to the dimensionless unit u.I otherwise.
